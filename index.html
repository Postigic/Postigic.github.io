    About Me

    I started programming at 12 years old, learning JavaScript for Game Development. If I had to give an honest guess as to why, it was probably just to have an excuse to play games. I do vaguely remember how strangely exciting it felt to write a game and watch it come to life.

    It wasn't until I entered secondary school that it grew into something beyond just game development. I DSA'd (Direct School Admission) into the Infocomm Club, where I began participating in competitions, as well as learning Python, Lua, C, and C++ out of pure curiosity. By the time O-Level subject selection came around, Computing was the obvious pick.

    On paper, I graduated from Bukit View Secondary School, and am currently studying at Singapore Polytechnic for a Diploma in Computer Science.

    I still can't fully explain why it ended up being CS; I've honestly just enjoyed learning for the sake of it, and CS is what stuck. These days, I'm mostly focused on studying discrete mathematics and practicing proof writing. I'd like to end up somewhere more theoretical, but it's still vague.

    On an unrelated note, I was 14 when I first started developing this website, and it has gone through several iterations since, so please excuse some of the scuff. I'm currently in the midst of cleaning this up and my GitHub documentation, though I'm mostly sitting on the latter.

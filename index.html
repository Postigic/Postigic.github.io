    About Me

    I started programming at 12 years old, learning JavaScript for Game Development. If I had to give an honest guess as to why, it was probably just to have an excuse to play games. I do vaguely remember finding the feeling of writing a game and watching it come to life strangely exciting.

    It wasn't until I entered secondary school that it grew into something beyond just game development. I DSA'd (Direct School Admission) into the Infocomm Club, where I began participating in competitions and tried out Python, Lua, C, and C++ out of pure curiosity. Taking the O-Level Computing subject further solidified my interest.

    I still can't fully explain why it ended up being CS; I've honestly just enjoyed learning for the sake of it, and CS is what stuck. These days, I'm mostly focused on studying discrete mathematics and practicing proof writing. I'd like to end up somewhere more theoretical, but it's still vague.

    On paper, I graduated from Bukit View Secondary School, and am currently studying at Singapore Polytechnic for a Diploma in Computer Science.

    I started making this website when I was 14, and it has gone through several iterations since. I'm currently in the midst of cleaning this up and my GitHub documentation, though I'm mostly sitting on the latter.
